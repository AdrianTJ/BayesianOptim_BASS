## The three headline numbers (fixed; sources in parentheses)

1. **3 of 6** widely-deployed BO libraries silently re-spend ≥10% of an
   80-evaluation budget on already-evaluated categorical combinations
   beyond pigeonhole — 2/6 ≥25%, 1/6 ≥40%; max: optuna GPSampler median
   53/80 revisits (~27 unique configs) while posting the matrix's best
   solve record (H1-WASTE, H1-MASK).
2. The waste is **invisible in convergence curves** (bit-identical-curve
   figure from the controlled study; H1-MASK's solve-vs-waste split in
   the wild).
3. **Z = 3 of 4** benchmarks change their library ranking once duplicate
   handling is budget-equalized (H2-ZOFW).
