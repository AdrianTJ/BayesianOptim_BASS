## Findings (F1–F5, candidate ledger claims)

**F1 — Waste is machinery-determined, not quality-determined.** The
no-dedup group's excess: optuna-tpe +24.3 (d3), +28.0 (d5), +18.0 (d6),
+16.0 (pest); hyperopt-tpe +12.3/+13.0/+6.0/+7.0; optuna-gp
+32.3/+37.0/+1.0/0. The dedup group sits at ≈0 excess in every cell.
Solve rates do NOT sort the same way — see F2.

**F2 — Solving masks waste (the invisibility thesis, in the wild).**
optuna-gp solves cat_ackley_d3 25/25 and d5 25/25 — best solve record of
any library — while grinding 52–55 of its 80 evaluations into revisits
(unique configs: 25–28). A leaderboard shows a winner; the audit shows
two-thirds of its budget re-spent. It even solves d6_L11 (K=1.77M) on
13/25 seeds where nothing else manages >2. This is the strongest deployed
instance of the paper's central claim: convergence metrics are blind to
machinery waste.

**F3 — On the 5^25 space, any revisit is algorithmic.** Random collides
0 times in 25×80 evals (pigeonhole ≈1e-14). optuna-tpe still revisits
median 16/80 (20% of budget), hyperopt-tpe 7/80 — TPE's per-dimension
independent categorical sampling concentrates probability mass on
already-seen combinations even in an astronomically large space. All
GP/RF-family libraries: 0.

**F4 — One library refuses rather than revisits.** smac: 0 revisits in
all 150 runs, and on the smallest space (K=125) it raises
`ConfigurationSpaceExhaustedException` mid-run (6–8 of 25 seeds, at
59–75/80 evals) rather than propose a duplicate. A crash instead of
silent waste is a *defensible design choice made visible only by
auditing* — neither behavior is reported by convergence curves.

**F5 — Mixed-space null confirms the metric.** On func2C/func3C every
library shows 0 exact revisits (P7): the counter does not overfire where
continuous coordinates make exact repeats measure-zero. (Combination-level
waste on mixed spaces is a different quantity — H2's controlled-dedup
re-comparison territory, per the original article's Failure Mode II.)

## Deployment ironies (descriptive, for the paper's discussion)

- The most-installed default (optuna TPESampler) carries 20–35% excess
  waste; the library that handles duplicates best in our matrix (ax,
  0 revisits everywhere) is also the heaviest dependency; skopt — dedup
  detection built in — is archived.
- Waste ≠ failure: optuna-gp is simultaneously the most wasteful (F2) and
  the best categorical solver in the matrix. The protocol point is not
  "wasteful libraries lose" but "curves don't show what budget bought".

## Threats & limitations

- Single budget (80) and 25 seeds/cell; no per-cell significance tests
  were pre-registered (medians + IQR reported; the effect sizes — 0 vs
  52–55 of 80 — dwarf seed noise).
- Cat-Ackley's additive structure favors one-hot GP surrogates; optuna-gp
  solving d6_L11 13/25 likely exploits it. Benchmark-structure dependence
  of *solve* claims is real; *waste* claims (F1/F3) are unaffected.
- SMAC's exhaustion is not seed-stable across runs (2 of 8 crashed seeds
  completed on re-run) — environment-order dependence inside SMAC, not
  our harness; counting is unaffected.
- pest_control is our determinized variant (disclosed in benchmarks.py);
  revisit-as-waste on the stochastic original would need a
  noise-averaging argument we deliberately avoid.
- Wrapper fairness: defaults only; non-defaults recorded per run in
  results.jsonl (seed, cosmetic logging, smac deterministic=True, ax
  one-trial-per-ask; is_ordered=True is ax's own default for int choices).
